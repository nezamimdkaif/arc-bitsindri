Recruitment Verticals

Choose Your Engineering Focus

We recruit motivated students across all branches and academic years of BIT Sindri.

Mechanical & Aerospace

CAD 3D modeling (SolidWorks/Fusion 360), FEA structural simulation, rocker-bogie kinematics, CNC machining, 3D printing, and rocket airframe composite lamination.

Electronics & Avionics

Custom PCB design (KiCad), microcontroller firmware (STM32, Teensy, ESP32), sensor fusion (IMU, Baro, GPS), power distribution, and LoRa long-range telemetry.

Autonomous AI & Software

Linux & ROS 2 development, ArduPilot flight stacks, computer vision (OpenCV, YOLO), LiDAR SLAM, autonomous path planning, and ground station telemetry software.

Operations & Outreach

Corporate sponsorship outreach, budget management, technical documentation, graphic design, social media storytelling, and national event logistics.
