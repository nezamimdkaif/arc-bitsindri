ISRO Robotics Challenge (IRoC-U 2024 & 2026)

Rover simulation on Martian terrain
Conducted by:
Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) & U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), Bengaluru.
Problem Statement (PS):
Design, manufacture, and operate an extraterrestrial planetary exploration rover capable of traversing simulated lunar/martian craters and boulder fields, executing autonomous obstacle avoidance, and collecting regolith core samples.
How We Performed & Result:
Team ARC engineered a full-scale 6-wheel rocker-bogie planetary rover with custom compliant wheels, differential pivot, and onboard autonomous perception. Ranked among the Top 12 teams nationwide across India in IRoC-U 2024 and achieved National Semi-Finalist standing in IRoC-U 2026.
Top 12 in India (2024) • National Semi-Finalist (2026)
NATIONAL FINALISTS

IN-SPACe Model Rocketry Challenge 2025

Conducted by:
Indian National Space Promotion and Authorization Centre (IN-SPACe), Department of Space, Govt. of India.
Problem Statement (PS):
Design, aerodynamically simulate in OpenRocket, fabricate, and launch a single-stage solid-motor model sounding rocket (K-motor class) reaching an apogee of ~1.1 km while deploying an operational CANSAT telemetry payload and safely recovering via dual parachutes.
How We Performed & Result:
Under the name Team AADISHAKTI, ARC designed Sounding Rocket NANDIGHOSH (1.66m length, 15.6cm airframe). Selected as National Finalists and attended the national rocketry workshop in Ahmedabad, presenting and launching prototype hardware in the presence of ISRO Chairman S. Somanath.
National Finalists • Prototype Flight at IN-SPACe Ahmedabad
NATIONAL CONTEST

SAE International Aerothon & Hackathons

Conducted by:
SAE India, Flipkart GRiD, & Bhartiya Antariksh Hackathon (ISRO).
Problem Statement (PS):
Design and optimize high-efficiency autonomous aerial UAV platforms meeting strict payload drop accuracy, aerodynamic endurance, structural safety factors, and mission autonomous control parameters.
How We Performed & Result:
Team ARC executed comprehensive CAD and CFD aerodynamic simulations, carbon composite airframe fabrication, and successful flight demonstration rounds against premier national engineering colleges.
